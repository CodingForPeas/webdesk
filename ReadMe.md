## Raison D'etre
I know, I know, there are umpteen other homelab interfaces, often a damn site better than this one, so I wanted to answer this question first.
I was fed up of editing configuration files, having fixed or constrained layouts, amongst a gazillion other minor grievances. I wanted a multiuser interface that I could add things as I went along, without any hassle. Individual users could tailor what they see and how they see it based on their needs without affecting other users - especially me :)
Whilst this is still work in progress, with a few issues, I hope to keep it simple as it is, after all, a place to launch from to get to other places - not a place to linger.
